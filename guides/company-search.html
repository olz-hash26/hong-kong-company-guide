How to Search for a Company in Hong Kong

Hong Kong · Practical guide · Updated November 2026

A Hong Kong company search should do more than return a name. The key task is confirming that the result refers to the legal entity you intend to research and understanding the limits of the information shown.

Start with the legal name

Search using the full legal name, including its legal ending where known. A brand, trading name or abbreviation may differ from the name recorded in the company register. Try reasonable spelling and spacing variations, but do not treat a similar name as proof of identity.

Use a registration identifier

A company number can help distinguish entities with similar names. Enter it carefully and check that you are using the right kind of identifier; company registration and business registration references serve different purposes.

Compare the returned details

Check the legal name and other available particulars against the documents or information supplied by the business. Consider the date of the record and whether later filings may have changed the details.

Verify important findings

For formal decisions, obtain or inspect the relevant official record or document through the Companies Registry. A third-party search service can help with discovery, but it should not replace official verification.

Key point: Keep an audit trail

For easier discovery of Hong Kong company information, visit CompanySearch.com.hk. Use the relevant official registry service for formal verification and original documents.
