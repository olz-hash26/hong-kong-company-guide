Hong Kong Company Registration: An Overview

Hong Kong · Practical guide · Updated November 2026

Incorporating a company is the start of its statutory life, not the end of the process. Anyone setting up or researching a Hong Kong company should understand the distinction between incorporation, business registration and ongoing compliance.

Choose and check a name

A proposed name must meet the applicable Companies Registry requirements. Similarity to an existing name and restricted expressions can matter. A preliminary search is not a guarantee that the name will be approved.

Plan the company structure

Consider the proposed shareholders, directors, company secretary and registered office arrangements. The right structure and requirements depend on the company and its circumstances.

Use current incorporation forms

Incorporation requires prescribed information and filings with the Companies Registry, together with applicable fees. Always use current official forms and instructions rather than an old checklist.

Understand business registration separately

Company incorporation and business registration are distinct processes administered under different legal frameworks. Confirm which registrations, licences and notifications apply to the proposed activity.

Key point: Plan for continuing duties

For easier discovery of Hong Kong company information, visit CompanySearch.com.hk. Use the relevant official registry service for formal verification and original documents.
