Hong Kong Company Number: What It Is and How to Use It

Hong Kong · Practical guide · Updated November 2026

Company names can be similar, amended or displayed differently across documents. A company number is a useful reference for distinguishing legal entities, provided it is copied accurately and matched to the right record.

Why the number matters

A registration identifier helps separate one legal entity from another. It is particularly useful when researching companies with similar names or matching records from different sources.

Do not confuse different references

A company may have both a company registration number and a business registration number. They are not interchangeable. Check the issuing authority and the label on the source document.

Match the number to the entity

After searching, compare the returned legal name and other available details with the company you are investigating. Do not rely on a number copied from an old invoice or an unverified directory entry.

Record the source and date

When using an identifier in a report, note where it came from and when it was checked. This is useful when records change or a review must be repeated.

Key point: Use a company number as a precise search key, then validate the result against official registry information.
